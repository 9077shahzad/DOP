# DOP field mapping — implementation review (29 September 2026)

| Information | Customer wizard | Anfrage | Offerte | Auftrag | PDF / risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer/contact and invoice address | `PlannerState` | `leads.service_details.customer_address` | `quotes.customer_*` | quote linked by reference | Offer PDF uses quote fields; default to work address only when no different billing address. |
| Work addresses, access and distance | `PlannerState` | `leads.service_details.origin/destination` | `quotes.from_*/to_*`, `services.access/places`, confirmed km | `jobs_execution.origin/destination`, linked quote | Quote PDF from linked quote; current order JSON only contains street and postcode, losing access for standalone consumers. |
| Inventory/volume | `PlannerState.items`, inventory catalogue | `service_details.items` | `quotes.items`, calculated volume | `jobs_execution.inventory`, linked quote | Configured catalogue volumes now feed the calculator for formerly unpriced items; missing volume leaves the request without an estimate. Liegestuhl is absent from the catalogue and requires PS Umzug configuration. |
| Service flags, extras, room/property | `PlannerState` | `service_details` | `quotes.services`, `room_units`, `property_type` | `jobs_execution.service_key/extras`, linked quote | Cleaning checklist and room details are not copied into the standalone job row. |
| Cleaning, disposal, dates and notes | `PlannerState` | `service_details.cleaning/disposal/clean_date` | `quotes.services.cleaning/disposal/clean_date`, `move_date`, `customer_notes` | linked quote; `booking_notes` | Cleaning-only staff summary is condensed; linked quote remains needed for full details. Verify disposal notes across every document stage. |
| Uploads | uploaded privately, linked after intake | `lead_attachments` | `lead_attachments` and `quote_attachments` | `jobs_execution.attachments`, linked quote | Offer and order dossiers list both attachment sources and provide short-lived signed view/download links; sending attachments and historical document audit still need verification. |
| Teams | inferred estimate (`crew`) | estimated crew in `service_details` | `quotes.crew` (move estimate), cleaning duration computed | dispatch team on job | Separate move/clean team assignments are not independently stored; do not invent headcount. |
| Price/version/acceptance | published configuration → estimate | estimated total | `quotes` and append-only `quote_versions`; sent/accepted gate | linked quote and value at conversion | Order PDF uses an order title and suppresses acceptance; printing is blocked when current and accepted version numbers differ. A matching version number alone does not prove the live quote content matches its versioned snapshot; immutable operational snapshot and full staff-only offer editing with audited recalculation remain open. |

Existing records remain readable through the linked quote; no historical row is rewritten merely to display additional details. A PDF remains a versioned commercial document, not an exact byte-for-byte copy of the customer wizard.
